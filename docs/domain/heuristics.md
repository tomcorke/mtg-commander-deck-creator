# App heuristics

Source review checked 2026-10-03; code-state notes updated 2026-10-25 after #125, #127, #128, and the [six-deck role audit](../role-detection-audit.md) (#33). Exact weights, formulas, quotas, and timings belong in the owning code, not this reference. A detected match is a clue; no match does not prove no interaction. See [rules](commander-rules.md), [policy](format-policy.md), and [provider contracts](data-sources.md) for stronger claims.

## Roles and targets

`src/deck-analysis.ts` owns editable defaults and overlapping classifiers; wipe matches suppress targeted-removal matches. Builder counts include commanders, not sideboard cards. **No target is a construction minimum.**

`cardFeatures` retains rules-text evidence, explicit activated/additional-cost text, draw beneficiaries, and unknown matches that get no role credit. Its strategy clues can overlap: resources (mana, card flow, tokens), enablers (selected sacrifice outlets, graveyard casting, trigger multipliers), payoffs (selected player-controlled triggers), and explicit game-winning finishers. Unrecognized strategy text is unknown, not evidence that a card lacks a purpose. These are narrow heuristics, not an Oracle-text parser. Costs are recorded, not evaluated for affordability; conditions, resource supply, and most implicit finishers remain unmodeled. Reviewed Oracle-ID overrides correct individual cards without widening the rules. The six-deck audit retains conservative no-credit states for limited edicts and expensive land-to-Treasure conversion; its fixture preserves independent labels where the app review chose narrower scope.

Heuristic update (#626, 2026-11-18): cycling and discard-to-draw costs are selection, not draw-role credit. Transforming and meld cards use only front-face text for all feature roles: the reverse requires setup that role targets do not model. Missing front-face text earns no feature credit. Modal DFCs keep both playable faces; land-role detection is unchanged. Scoring version 14 invalidates prior rankings.

| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

Never call this count available land drops or a required land count.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

Never call it guaranteed clearance or required wipes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

### Ramp counting (#592)

Heuristic, decided 2026-11-15. The ramp target counts mana a deck can use again without doing anything else first. `manaFeature` classifies each rules-text ability that adds mana or creates Treasure, then keeps the strongest kind: repeatable, then conditional, then burst.

| Kind          | Counts as ramp | Wording                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Examples                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `land-ramp`   | Yes            | A land search or land card onto the battlefield                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Cultivate, Myriad Landscape                                                                              |
| `repeatable`  | Yes            | An activated ability whose cost does not sacrifice, exile or discard the card itself; a granted ability that is one; a "whenever" or "at the beginning" trigger with no condition below, including one that makes a Spawn-style token; a tax an opponent can decline; "lands you control produce an additional" doublers | Sol Ring, Skirk Prospector, Cryptolith Rite, Smothering Tithe, Black Market Connections, Awakening Zone  |
| `conditional` | No             | A repeating trigger whose text before the mana or Treasure names attacking, blocking, combat damage, a sacrifice or a death, or contains "if"; an activated ability with "if" before the effect                                                                                                                          | Ragavan, Crime Novelist, Enterprising Scallywag, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker's token, Pitiless Plunderer |
| `burst`       | No             | Instants and sorceries; "when" triggers (enters, dies, cast), including one-shot token makers; costs that sacrifice, exile or discard the card itself; Saga chapters; loyalty minus abilities                                                                                                                            | Brightstone Ritual, Big Score, Wily Goblin, Reckless Lackey, Spawn-Gang Commander, Elvish Spirit Guide   |

Burst and conditional mana keep their labels and the `resource` strategy clue, so themes and Deck Doctor still see them. They fill no ramp role, so they no longer meet the ramp target or earn ramp deck-need credit. `roleCredit` no longer gives burst half ramp credit. Before #592, burst counted fully toward the target, and the cost-colon and trigger checks read Reckless Lackey, Crime Novelist, Enterprising Scallywag and Fable as repeatable.

- **Limits:** "Whenever you cast" triggers (Storm-Kiln Artist) count as repeatable, though they depend on casting spells. "If" is a blunt test: trivial intervening conditions read as conditional, so Blinkmoth Urn keeps repeatable through a reviewed override, and Carpet of Flowers does not. Death triggers depend on the deck's creatures dying, which sacrifice decks supply. Aura and enchantment doublers worded "its controller adds an additional" (Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl, Mana Reflection) are not detected, as before #592. Mana amounts, costs and spending restrictions are not evaluated.
- **Six-deck audit:** Carrier Thrall and Dockside Extortionist keep their independent ramp labels in `independent_expected`, with an `app_review` note. Mystic Intellect's ramp count falls from 10 to 9; the other five decks are unchanged.
- **Scryfall Oracle bulk, 2026-11-14:** of 32,643 Commander-legal cards, ramp-role cards fell from 1,543 to 1,239. 329 left the role: 236 that were already burst, 77 now conditional, 14 now burst (mostly loyalty, self-sacrifice and Spirit Guide abilities) and 2 now unknown. 25 joined it, previously unknown because of cross-sentence or modal wording, such as Smothering Tithe, Black Market Connections and Deathrite Shaman.
- **Pinned builds:** see [treasure-ramp-592.md](../treasure-ramp-592.md). In Krenko, the target fills at batch 15 instead of 7, and Sol Ring is offered. Rabbit and Tatyova are unchanged.
- **Land guidance:** the land estimate reads the same ramp count. A deck that relied on Treasure and rituals now reads as having less ramp, so its land range rises by about a third of a land per card removed from the count. Karsten's `c` (see the land-count bullets below) is unchanged, because it never used this app's ramp role.

## Curve and mana estimates

`src/deck-analysis.ts` owns these calculations; `src/app/useBuilderData.ts` consumes them.

- **Curve/types:** land-classified cards, including spell/land MDFCs, leave the spell curve; transforming DFCs use their front face for land/type classification. Fractional values are floored into display buckets; types overlap and sections use display priority. Multiple options and alternative costs distort demand. Never call the curve actual casting costs or exhaustive playable options.
- **Land range:** `estimateLandGuidance` starts at `landGuidanceBaseline` (38) and adjusts for spell average (including commanders), the share of spells at mana value 5+, and detected ramp and draw. Below 40 nonland spells, a partial deck scales every adjustment by `n / 40`: role counts project to 64 spells blended toward 10, and the average and 5+ adjustments shrink toward the baseline (their reasons say "N% weight until 40 spells"). The centre is clamped to `landGuidanceCentreLimits` (34–42). The range then widens (±3 under 20 spells, ±2 under 50, else ±1) and is clamped to `landGuidanceRangeLimits` (32–44). The builder always shows the range with each adjustment. Unlike ramp cards are treated equally; fixing, reductions, land modes, MDFCs and strategy are absent, and the coefficients are uncalibrated, so it can over- or under-prescribe lands. Never present it as an official recommendation or optimum. See [land-guidance-379.md](../land-guidance-379.md).
  - **Baseline and limits (#587, 2026-11-14):** both sources are heuristics. EDHREC's 2022 decklist distribution has a median of 36 lands and 5th–95th percentiles of 30–41; it describes decklists, not optimal play. Karsten's 2022 Commander formula, `31.42 + 3.13·m − 0.28·c`, gives about 38 at `m = 3` and `c = 10` and about 40.8 at `c = 0`. It scales 60-card data and is not fitted to Commander decks. Its `c` counts cheap (mana value 2 or less) draw and ramp spells by Karsten's own Oracle-text classifier, not this app's ramp and draw roles, so the app's assumed 10 ramp and 10 draw do not map onto `c = 10`. The baseline borrows the result for a typical deck; it does not apply the formula. Source and derivation: `knowledge/topics/deckbuilding/commander-land-counts.md` in the MTG knowledge base, section "Worked derivation: 38 lands at m = 3 and c = 10". The coefficients were left unchanged, so every output moved up by 3 lands before clamping. Karsten's mean-MV slope (about 3.13 lands per point) is steeper than ours (2); #98 owns calibrating the baseline, the limits and that slope. `defaultDeckTargets.lands` is the baseline, so new decks start at 38. Saved decks keep their stored target, because a saved 35 cannot be told apart from an edited one.
  - **Partial-deck weight check (#576, 2026-11-14):** 13 distinct complete #98 eval decks (Krenko, Tatyova, Kozilek and Ayula builds in local research storage), card data from the 2026-11-12 local card index. For each deck, 300 random partial decks per size kept the commander and added lands in proportion. The table shows mean distance from the full deck's centre, then the share of partial centres at the 40-land clamp (measured at baseline 35, centre limits 32–40, before #587). `n / 40` matches the ramp and draw blend and was kept: `n / 64` and `(n / 40)²` were within about 0.1 lands of it at every size, and `√(n / 40)` was worse. Random order is not real build order, and the full deck's centre is itself the uncalibrated heuristic, so this checks stability, not correctness.

    | Nonland spells         | 1         | 10        | 20       | 40       |
    | ---------------------- | --------- | --------- | -------- | -------- |
    | Unscaled (before #576) | 4.1 / 46% | 2.5 / 13% | 1.6 / 5% | 0.8 / 0% |
    | `n / 40` (current)     | 1.5 / 0%  | 1.5 / 0%  | 1.1 / 0% | 0.8 / 0% |
- **Colour demand/supply:** counts coloured, `{C}`, and `{S}` symbols across nonland faces against reported colourless output and snow sources. Alternative faces and hybrid/Phyrexian symbols can overstate demand; conditional sources/commanders overstate early supply, while multi-mana producers/reducers can be understated. Never call these available mana, casting probabilities, or identity validation.
- **Basic-land plan:** `src/domain/land-plan.ts` (#589) plans the land slots left toward the editable target for both **Choose lands** and **Build a starting deck**, so the two never disagree. Nonbasics come first (see [Land plan](#land-plan)); basics fill the rest, split by colour demand against land sources only, proportionally to pips, evenly without demand, using largest remainders. Spells that make mana do not count as sources, so mana creatures and rocks never crowd out a colour's basics. Each identity colour with no basic in the deck gets at least one while slots allow. Empty identity selects Wastes. Activation costs, conditional demand, and missing chosen-colour state make the split unreliable. Never call it a solved or optimal mana base, or proof of a colourless choice.

## Land roles

`src/domain/land-roles.ts` (#588) reads what a nonbasic land is for. The [land plan](#land-plan) (#589) consumes it; #590 may widen the candidate pool. **Never call a role match proof the land does that job well.** A match shows the wording that fired, not the strength, cost or reliability of the effect.

`detectLandRoles(card, identity)` returns each role with the sentence or type line that matched. It reads the land face of a spell/land MDFC, strips reminder text and reads the card's own name as "this land". Entering tapped and costing life are drawbacks, not roles.

| Role                    | Detects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Known misses and over-counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fixing**              | Two or more identity colours from Scryfall `produced_mana`, basic land types on the type line (CR 305.6, so shocks and typed duals count without printed mana text), or a "search your library for" fetch naming basic types or any basic land. | `produced_mana` lists every colour for conditional or chosen-colour lands (Cavern of Souls, Exotic Orchard, Temple of the Dragon Queen), so they count. Landcycling (Ash Barrens) is only flexible. Lands that grant types to other lands (Urborg) are not fixing.                                                                  |
| **Utility ability**     | A non-mana activated ability, or a triggered ability, that is not another role's line, a fetch, bounce or recursion.                                                                                                                            | Static abilities are skipped except by override. Minor riders (gain life, scry or surveil, 1 damage, {E}) and triggered drawbacks (self-damage, karoo and depletion upkeep, self-sacrifice) are excluded. Costs and conditions are not judged, so weak or expensive abilities count.                                                |
| **Creature-land**       | "This land becomes … creature", a land creature type line, or a transforming land whose back face is a creature.                                                                                                                                | Lands that animate other lands, or crew and saddle, do not count.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Flexible slot**       | Spell/land MDFCs (the spell face), cycling and landcycling, channel, transmute and morph.                                                                                                                                                       | Other modes, such as hideaway or adventure-style alternatives, are missed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Land synergy**        | Fetching a land onto the battlefield, sacrificing itself, bounce lands, returning land cards from a graveyard (focus `lands-matter`), and each CR 205.3i nonbasic land type on its type line or named in its text (focus is the type).          | Every chosen self-sacrifice counts, including utility sacrifices; one forced by a drawback trigger (Glimmervoid, City of Traitors) does not. Land types come from the knowledge-base list below, not Scryfall's catalog.                                                                                                            |
| **Theme or keyword**    | A creature type in its text (focus is that theme), "choose a creature type" (`Chosen type`), artifact and snow type lines or text, graveyard-, counters- (`+1/+1`, proliferate, graft) and spells-matter text.                                  | Every-type text (Mutavault) is not a theme match, as in #174. Creature-lands that become a type match that type; a negated type ("non-Human") does not. Spells-matter needs instant, sorcery or noncreature wording. Graveyard hate and "put into a graveyard from the battlefield" are not graveyard themes; surveil taplands are. |
| **Interaction or hate** | Graveyard exile (the Expected opponents graveyard clue), land destruction, tapping lands, and opponent-side restrictions ("opponents can't", "enter tapped", "don't untap", "cost {N} more").                                                   | Creature removal on a land is utility, not interaction. Other stax wording is missed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Colourless utility**  | No coloured mana or basic land type, plus a utility, interaction or creature-land match; or a mana ability adding more than one `{C}` (Ancient Tomb, Tron).                                                                                     | "Strong" is not judged: any matching ability counts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Lands whose only purpose is scaling coloured mana (Cabal Coffers, Gaea's Cradle, Serra's Sanctum, Nykthos) get no role; see [#596](https://github.com/tomcorke/mtg-commander-deck-creator/issues/596). Reviewed Oracle-ID overrides (`landRoleOverrides`) correct single cards, each with the Oracle text checked: they add static utility (Urborg, Yavimaya, Reliquary Tower), static stax (The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale) and a typal clue (Path of Ancestry). A chosen colour scaled by devotion or a count ("Choose a color. Add an amount of mana of that color": Nykthos, Three Tree City) is not fixing, although Scryfall `produced_mana` lists every colour.

**Land types.** `nonbasicLandTypes` holds the 12 nonbasic land types of CR 205.3i, Comprehensive Rules effective 2026-11-06, from the [knowledge base](../../../mtg-knowledge-base/knowledge/topics/commander/rules.md#land-types-2053i-30558) (checked 2026-11-14). Scryfall's catalog adds Cloud from a playtest card; it is left out. Recheck with each new rules file.

**Deck evidence.** `deckLandRoles` returns the roles a deck uses. Core roles (fixing, utility, creature-land, flexible, interaction, colourless utility) always apply. The issue lists creature-land as neither core nor earned; it needs no deck evidence, so it is core. Fixing's weight is `(colours − 1) / 4`: 0 for mono-coloured and colourless decks, 1 for five colours. The earned roles, land synergy and theme, apply only for a focus the deck earned, and each focus keeps its evidence:

- a selected theme or extra theme mapped to the focus (Landfall → `lands-matter`; Graveyard, Recursion or Mill → Graveyard; Counters or +1/+1 counters → Counters; Spellslinger or Spell copying → Spellslinger; Artifacts; a creature-type theme);
- a commander sentence matching the focus (landfall, land graveyard or land sacrifice wording for `lands-matter`; the theme's tag pattern; a creature type; snow; a land type);
- 3 or more noncommander main-deck cards (`landRoleDeckThreshold`), anywhere in the deck. Theme focuses use `hasTag` on tags read from nonland cards' rules text only, so mana rocks do not earn Artifacts and creatures do not earn their own types; graveyard hate does not count for Graveyard. Snow and land types have no theme tag, so they read text: nonland cards for snow, and any card for a land type, since Gate and Locus payoffs are often lands.

`deckLandRoleMatches` keeps a land's core matches and only the earned matches whose focus the deck earned; a `Chosen type` land fits any earned creature-type focus. Broad theme tags (`artifact`, `graveyard`) earn focuses easily, and a single commander mention is enough. EDHREC `lands`, `utilitylands` and `manafixing` membership may later rank lands as a popularity signal; it never grants a role, and Scryfall Tagger is not used for role credit.

**Corpus check (2026-11-14).** Over the 1,168 Commander-legal nonbasic lands in Scryfall Oracle bulk data 2026-11-04, with a five-colour identity, lands per role: fixing 672, utility 417, land synergy 313, theme 285, colourless utility 210, flexible 106, creature-land 51, interaction 19; 37 had no role. Sampled matches were mostly genuine; the no-role lands were mainly scaling mana, depletion and storage lands, banding lands, minor-rider taplands and static abilities. The overrides above came from this review.

## Land plan

`planLandFill` in `src/domain/land-plan.ts` (#589) decides how many land slots go to nonbasics, which ones and in what order. Choose lands and the starting deck both call it. Every number below is an app heuristic; #98 owns checking them. **Never call the plan a solved, optimal or recommended mana base.**

- **Candidates:** lands from the queue and the Later list, minus deck, sideboard, ignored and Later cards, filtered by the price cap, Core fast mana, Only-sets and ownership modes, and the manual Add construction and Game Changer checks (`landFillCandidates` in `src/domain/bulk-add.ts`). The same check runs again against the planned lands (`landPlanAllowed`), so two Game Changer lands never both fill the last Game Changer slot. The input is a plain list, so #590 can add per-role searches without changing the planner. A land is a candidate only with a role the deck can use (`deckLandRoleMatches`); lands with no role, such as scaling-mana lands (#596), are never planned.
- **Nonbasic cap** (`nonbasicLandCap`): 6 for mono-coloured and colourless identities, 10 for two colours, 15 for three or more. Nonbasic lands already in the deck use it up.
- **Basic reserve:** the larger of 1 basic per identity colour (`basicReservePerColour`) and 2 per basic-fetching card (`basicReservePerFetcher`). A basic-fetching card is a land, or a nonland card `cardFeatures` reads as `land-ramp` or `land-to-hand`, whose "search your library for" text names basic lands or a basic land type. A planned land that fetches basics (Evolving Wilds style) raises the reserve too. Basics already in the deck count toward it. The ramp role count is unchanged; the reserve only reads it.
- **Fill order:** (1) **fixing**, re-ranked after each pick by `sourceGaps` (pips at mana value 3 or less count double; land sources only), while a land adds two or more colours still short of sources and its gap score, less its drawback penalties, beats the basic it would replace. When only one colour is short a basic does as well, so fixing stops. (2) **Earned roles**, land synergy and theme or keyword, only for a focus the deck earned (#588). (3) **Utility**, creature-land, flexible, interaction and colourless utility. Within a step lands rank by role score (the sum of the deck's role weights, less drawbacks), then EDHREC inclusion, then name. Basics fill every slot left.
- **Tapped lands follow Power** (`tappedTolerance`): an always-tapped land loses 0.1 (Core), 0.3 (Upgraded) or 0.6 (High) against a role worth 1, and the deck holds at most 8, 4 or 1 of them, counting ones already in it. A land that enters tapped only sometimes (shocks, checklands) loses a fixed 0.15; a land that costs life loses 0.1. Tapped detection is the same text test as before; untapping conditions are not judged.
- **Colourless-only lands** (no identity colour or basic type, and no fetch of one; a search for "a basic land card" counts as every identity colour): at most 3 for a mono-coloured deck, 2 for two colours and 1 for three or more, plus the deck's `{C}` source gap. A colourless identity has no limit.
- **Budget, unchanged (#498, #492):** the max price drops lands. With a deck budget, a land that would break it is left out of the plan, listed last and marked **Over budget**; the player can still tick it. The starting deck also keeps the price of a basic for every empty slot after each pick, so spending stops while basics can still fill the deck. Choose lands keeps none back, since basics are not priced until they load.
- **Alternatives:** up to 6 usable lands the plan left out, each with why: over budget, the nonbasic cap, a slot kept for a basic, the tapped-land limit, the colourless-land limit, a check a manual Add would fail after the planned lands (such as the Game Changer limit), or a basic does as well.

Choose lands shows the plan grouped by role: Fixing, a group per earned focus ("Landfall and land synergy", "Gate synergy", "Typal", "Artifacts theme"), then Utility, Creature lands, Flexible slots, Interaction and Colourless utility. Planned lands start ticked; unticking one gives its slot to basics and the colour split and slot count update at once. Each tile quotes the matched rules text or type line, then why the deck wants the role (the colours it adds, a chosen theme, the commander's quoted sentence or a count of deck cards), and its drawbacks. The dialog labels this **Land plan** as a heuristic, like theme-fit reasons (#501). One confirm adds every ticked nonbasic and the basic split.

Known limits: the caps and penalties are uncalibrated; a role match says nothing about how strong or reliable the effect is; fixing measures sources against a proportional share of the target, not casting probabilities, so in a two- or three-colour deck with enough duals fixing usually fills the cap and earned roles get slots only through lands that also fix; landcycling and other land-to-hand searches count as fetchers whether or not they ramp; and the plan reads only the queue and Later list, so a missing role may mean no candidate was queued (#590).

## Role urgency

`src/deck-analysis.ts` starts guidance at **70 cards** and strengthens it at **85**; mana-role deficits receive extra weight. `src/app/recommendation-context.ts` gates health weighting. Overlapping roles exaggerate slot pressure; early gaps and misclassified functions can be missed. Never call urgency a deadline, legality failure, or proof of unplayability. Separate Thematic health suggestions in `src/app/useBuilderData.ts` are alternatives, never mandatory picks.

## Themes and pair clues

`src/domain/recommendation-themes.ts` tags wording/types and maps EDHREC aliases. Unsupported mechanics/slugs remain unsupported; generic Tribal is not selectable. Words can describe opponents or costs: `each player` need not be helpful Group hug, and sacrifice wording need not be an engine. Implicit interactions and new wording are missed. Never present tags as executed abilities, complete strategy classification, or official themes.

Theme-fit reasons (#501) quote the first rules-text sentence, or the type line, that matched a selected theme (`themeEvidence`). The quote shows why the tag fired, not that the card advances the plan; patterns spanning sentences fall back to a plain "Fits your … theme". The sidebar **theme cards** row counts main-deck matches like Deck review and starts from a target of **25**, an app estimate of about a quarter of the deck, not a community standard. The target is display only and never changes scoring. Never call it a required count or a measure of theme quality.

**Generic pick** (#560): in Theme first, with at least one theme chosen, an offer whose tags match none of the selected themes (`hasTag`, the test theme fit and theme cards use, so changelings count for creature-type themes) carries a "Generic pick" mark, labelled as a heuristic. `explainRecommendation` in `src/app/useBuilderData.ts` sets it; theme-fit offers never get it. "Generic" means only that no theme tag fired. The mark ignores roles, EDHREC inclusion and card quality, so it does not tell a staple from a niche off-theme card, and it never changes ranking. Missed wording marks cards that do serve the theme; broad tags leave generic cards unmarked. Never call it a staple verdict, an off-theme proof or a cut suggestion.

**On-theme alternative** (#563): beside a nonland generic pick with a detected role (ramp, card draw, targeted removal or board wipe, from `rolesForCard`), the offer names one card from the queue past the batch or the Later list that has the same role and matches a selected theme (`hasTag`). `findThemeAlternative` in `src/domain/theme-alternative.ts` picks it: the offer's roles in detection order, then the pool in order (ranked queue first, then Later by return), skipping cards Add could not take now (`recommendationDataError` against the add board). `themeAlternativeFinder` in `src/app/useBuilderData.ts` builds the pool. Both checks are text heuristics: a match means the detectors agree, not that the cards do the job equally well, and the alternative is not ranked against the offer by power, cost or EDHREC share. Land cards are left out on both sides, even ones that also read as ramp or draw. Later entries the current pool or price cap excludes (`available: false`) are skipped, as the queue skips them. Undecided skips waiting to return, ignored cards and cards outside the queue are never searched, so a missing alternative does not mean none exists. It is display only and never changes ranking. Adding it keeps the batch and leaves the offer undecided. Never call it a better card, an upgrade or a replacement the deck needs.

## Expected opponents

`src/domain/opposing-strategies.ts` (#503) decides which selected opposing strategies a card's rules text answers, one sentence at a time with reminder text removed. Graveyard: exiling graveyards or graveyard cards, graveyard replacement effects, and locks on cards in graveyards. Artifacts: destroying or exiling artifacts or any nonland permanent, artifact edicts, and artifact ability locks. Tokens: board wipes that hit creatures or permanents (damage included) and effects that shrink every opposing creature's toughness. Combo: counterspells for spells or abilities, one-spell-a-turn rules, ability locks, trigger stoppers, and search or win prevention. Big creatures: size-independent targeted destroy, exile or theft, creature edicts, Pacifism-style auras, and non-damage, non-shrinking wipes. Spellslinger: counterspells for any, instant, sorcery or noncreature spells, untyped spell taxes, one-spell-a-turn rules, and punishers for noncreature spells. Effects that name your own permanents or cards are excluded where they would otherwise match; a cost or trigger condition naming them ("Whenever a creature you control dies, …") does not veto the effect after it. Noncreature-permanent removal is not a big-creature answer. A 2026-11-13 audit over the 32,643 commander-legal cards in the local index matched 305 graveyard, 751 artifact, 587 token, 489 combo, 1,543 big-creature and 374 spellslinger answers; sampled matches were mostly genuine. Known misses and over-counts: modal and multi-sentence effects, temporary theft, conditional and narrow counters, bounce, damage-based removal (deliberately not a big-creature answer), stax beyond the listed wording, and self-replacement effects with unusual wording. Never call a match a guaranteed answer, or no match proof a card is useless against the strategy.

Each selected strategy has a default target of **3** answers in the main deck, commanders included, an app estimate rather than a community standard. Deck review's Expected opponents section has a target stepper per strategy (#565), from 0 to 99, saved with the deck; a target edited back to 3 is stored as unedited, and a deselected strategy keeps its target for when it is chosen again. Scoring adds 6 for each selected strategy the card answers while the deck is below that strategy's target and 2 once it is met, capped at 10 and halved under Theme first. With no expected opponents the factor is 0, so existing recommendation orders are unchanged. Offers lead with "Answers … decks" and quote the matching sentence, after theme fit (#501), which keeps the lead; Deck review lists each strategy's count against the target.

Sub-theme inference uses **3 matches among the last 12** noncommander selections. `src/app/useBuilderData.ts` owns suggestions; dismissals wait for increased support. `src/domain/recommendation-queue.ts` owns active-selection limits. Recency exaggerates incidental packages or overlooks older ones. Never call the trigger sufficient support or a construction restriction.

`src/domain/commander-catalog.ts` and `src/app/recommendation-actions.ts` own curated commander/pair shortcuts and narrow theme searches. They omit valid commanders, pairs, and implicit themes. Scryfall theme search is one page ordered by EDHREC popularity, not commander synergy; its fallback uses a random comparator, which is not uniform. Never call these exhaustive eligibility, general pairing validation, or unbiased recommendations.

Start's broader section (#481) adds, below a curated theme's picks, one Scryfall page of commanders whose rules text or type matches the theme's search term (`broaderTerm` in `src/domain/commander-discovery.ts`). A word match is weaker than a pick: "sacrifice" also matches costs and unrelated effects. Label these results "not picked for this theme"; never show them under "Picked for".

Start's Popularity sort (#514) loads one Scryfall page in EDHREC rank order, then reorders those loaded commanders by EDHREC `num_decks` from colour-identity list pages (`orderByDeckCount` in `src/domain/commander-discovery.ts`). Scryfall's `edhrec_rank` ranks a card across every deck, so it is only the fallback. Commanders missing from their identity's list (it holds at most 100), or whose list failed, follow in rank order, and the grid heading says so. The grid keeps rank order until every loaded identity's list settles, then reorders once. Cost: one cached background EDHREC request per colour identity among the loaded commanders, throttled to one a second. An exact colour filter needs one; an unfiltered grid can need all 32, about 30 seconds before the reorder. Other sorts read only the shown page's identities. A commander with many decks but a rank outside the loaded page is not loaded. Never call this order EDHREC's own commander ranking.

Pair clues match blink/ETB, tokens/payoffs, counters, sacrifice/death, graveyard/recursion, or artifact-token/payoff wording. They ignore ownership, targets, costs, timing, once-per-turn limits, and resources; setup may be missing or the beneficiary wrong. Other interactions are missed. Never present a pair as a proven interaction, infinite combo, or bracket-combo detector.

## Signature enrichment

`src/domain/signature-recommendations.ts` owns profiles and evidence thresholds. Families cover counters, ETB/blink, sacrifice, tokens/populate, enchantments, artifacts, lifegain, graveyard/recursion, spellslinger, landfall, and equipment.

Seeds need **two other noncommander, nonland participants**; repeated entries need not be distinct identities. Engine tests strip parentheticals and opponent-trigger sentences, and reject incidental equipped/enchanted triggers. Theme match, multiplier wording, then fit rank seeds. These profiles admit incidental matches and miss unsupported engines/wording; excluding Smothering Tithe or Skullclamp is not a claim they cannot power decks. Never call selection a complete engine inventory or synergy proof.

A pass selects **up to 3 seeds**, **24 names per seed**, hence **72 names**. Qualifying ETB seeds can use commander pages; others use card pages. Selected ordinary lists require minimum observed support and positive association: raw lift on card pages, synergy on commander pages. The owning module defines cutoffs; sorting favours deck count or synergy respectively. Truncated/omitted lists hide niche/new candidates; popularity admits irrelevant ones. Never call these cutoffs significance, relevance guarantees, or rules interactions.

Incoming Energy/token checks require tagged peers and can count commanders, unlike seed participation. Creature-type-choice and Angel/Zombie-support wording is rejected; other checks use participation, counter-pair clues, or narrow sacrifice fodder. Incoherent packages can pass and useful typal cards fail. Never present these screens as necessary or sufficient support.

`src/app/signature-actions.ts` and `src/app/useSignatureRecommendations.ts` own rolling budgets, retries, and idle/visibility/complete-deck gates. Cached, cancelled-before-dispatch, or foreground-promoted work does not add a background charge; actual retries do. Budgets defer useful work and do not coordinate tabs. Never present them as provider allowances, relevance thresholds, or game timing.

## Scores, learning, and batches

`src/domain/recommendation-scoring.ts` and `src/domain/recommendation-types.ts` own factor weights, penalties, and bands. Evidence labels, themes, collection, preferences, needs, and mana proxies form a fit score; **45** is the Recommended threshold for the best visible candidate. Taxonomy drift changes source-label weights; overlapping tags inflate scores, sparse tags under-rank useful cards, and aggregate sources misjudge castability, especially slash-symbol costs. Never present scores as win probability, card power, bracket, cEDH suitability, or fun.

`src/domain/recommendation-queue.ts` learns from Add/Ignore/like choices, not intrinsic quality. Generic shared tags spread preferences to unrelated cards; unexpressed reasons are missed. Never present learned values as verified intent or quality.

The queue balances creature/mana picks, novelty, reason variety, and theme coverage; Theme first preserves identity, while Deck needs first omits ordinary diversity quotas. Supply and fallbacks can relax quotas or restore mana-penalized creatures. Quotas displace higher scores and fail with sparse pools. Never present them as optimal composition or casting bans. The per-batch mana slot holds `Land or mana` cards, which are lands only (#379): a land type line, so a land back face counts. A nonland mana source keeps its EDHREC list's reason (Sol Ring is `Mana Artifacts`, a mana creature `Creature synergy`) and competes with other spells; from the fallback pool, or in a draft saved before #379, it is `Mana support`. Before #379, literal `Add {` text sent Sol Ring and the Signets into the one land slot, where lands always outranked them. The label is not a ramp role or available mana.

**Role quality** (#379): while ramp, draw, removal or wipes are short, an off-theme card's deck-need credit scales with its EDHREC inclusion for this commander, from 60% of full credit at 0% to full at **40%** and above (`roleQuality` in `src/domain/recommendation-scoring.ts`). So Sol Ring (about 80%) leads Commander's Sphere (about 6%). Generic role-gap and local-index results without inclusion data get the credit for 20%. Cards on a chosen theme, lands and other cards without inclusion data, such as the fallback pool, keep full credit, and a met role gives no lift, so a staple is not forced once on-theme cards meet the target. Inclusion is popularity with one commander, not card power.

**Creature-type package** (#379): for a creature-type theme, `typalCounts` in `src/domain/strategy-packages.ts` counts members (creatures of the type, changelings included) and payoffs (chosen-type support such as Patchwork Banner or Herald's Horn, and text such as "Rabbits you control", "for each Rabbit on the battlefield" or "whenever another Rabbit"; "non-Rabbit" never counts). A payoff gains up to 8 package points while the deck has fewer than one per **3** members; a member gains points while payoffs exceed that share. The ratio is an app guess, not a measured optimum. Regex wording misses some payoffs and counts some token makers' text, and lords that are also members count on both sides. Deck Doctor findings ignore this package.

The same module owns Later/undecided cooldowns and can advance to deferred cards when unseen cards run out. These are queue delays, never turns, elapsed-time waits, or usefulness judgments.

## Bulk adds and starting decks

`src/domain/bulk-add.ts` owns **Add all** and **Build a starting deck** (#492). Add all applies the same Add check to each undecided card in the visible batch and skips cards that fail it. A starting deck fills unmet nonland role targets from the ranked queue, then the remaining nonland slots in queue order, then the [land plan](#land-plan) Choose lands shows, then basics for every slot left. Each planned land is re-checked against the growing deck and budget before it is taken. Candidates are the queue and the Later list, minus ignored, Later and deck cards, filtered again by price cap, Core fast mana and Only-sets mode. Every pick passes the Add construction and Game Changer checks. With a deck budget, a pick fits only if basics could still fill every slot after it, so spending stops before the budget; unpriced cards always fit. The basic split counts only lands as existing sources, as Choose lands now does, so mana creatures and rocks do not crowd out a colour's basics. When candidates run out, extra basics reach 100 and the draft reports how many. Role counts use `rolesForCard`, so the role limits above apply. A starting deck is a ranked fill, not deck construction advice: it ignores curve shape, card quality beyond rank, synergy between picks and interaction balance. Never call it tuned, optimised, bracket-checked or a recommended final list. Undo removes each recorded copy from its board and restores the queue snapshot when the queue has not moved; otherwise it returns the cards and re-ranks. The record is session-only, like Deck Doctor history.

## Decision undo and Later & ignored

`src/domain/decision-undo.ts` owns undo for the last **Add**, **Later** or **Ignore** (#493). The record reuses the bulk-add copy accounting, so undoing an Add removes only the copy it placed. In the same batch, undo restores the card's previous decision, More like this and ignore reason. **Next recommendations** or applied settings commit the batch and stamp the record with the batch before the commit and the preference change this card taught. If nothing has changed since (same queue, waiting cards and batch number, no new decisions), undo puts that batch back, preferences included, and clears the card's decision; current card objects are kept and newly fetched cards stay queued after it. Otherwise undo subtracts the card's preference change, lifts the ignore, Later wait or added copy, and appends the card to the queue to be ranked at the next batch. The card does not return to the visible batch. A new decision replaces the record, a bulk add clears it, and it is session-only.

Later & ignored lists explicit Later choices (`DeferredCard.later`, or a wait with no undecided skip in saves older than the flag) and ignored names not decided in the current batch. Ignored cards' data is saved as `ignoredDetails`; older saves have names only, so those rows offer only Un-ignore. Add uses the batch's Add check. Bring back and Un-ignore append the card to the queue; Move to Later waits four batches. These list actions keep learned preferences: only Undo takes back what a decision taught.

## Power and collection preferences

- **Power presets:** `src/app/useRecommendationState.ts` and `src/app/useAppActions.ts` set optional exclusions. Loose bracket mappings over-filter acceptable Upgraded decks and miss combos, land denial, loops, and intent. Never present settings as bracket compliance.
- **Fast mana:** Core-only `preconFastMana` in `src/domain/recommendation-scoring.ts` is incomplete: it omits Sol Ring, Ancient Tomb, and Lion's Eye Diamond and includes outright-banned cards. Never call it the ban list, Game Changers list, or a complete definition.
- **Tutor/turn filters:** `excludedByOptions` in `src/domain/recommendation-sources.ts` is the shared eligibility check for EDHREC, Scryfall, set-based results, signature enrichment, and Doctor replacements/commander suggestions. Remote queries intentionally omit tutor/extra-turn Tagger exclusions: a different provider taxonomy must not discard cards before the local policy runs. The local policy exempts recognized land-only searches, including named basic land types; it detects selected singular/plural extra-turn grants and ignores selected prevention/replacement wording. Missing release dates are unknown and fail the unreleased-card exclusion. Broader remote set counts can exceed the locally eligible pool. Other wording, indirect engines, and loops escape. Never call these exhaustive, Tagger-equivalent, or complete bracket checks.
- **Set selection:** curated/selected sets in `src/domain/recommendation-types.ts` are affinity, not inventory or complete franchise taxonomy. Broad membership admits off-theme cards; other sets and allowed alternate printings are missed, especially when only the hydrated printing is checked. Never present matches as ownership or exhaustive theme coverage.
- **Automatic printing:** `selectedPrintingIndex` in `src/app/printing-actions.ts` keeps a manual or restored printing, then takes a collection-set or preferred-set printing. Otherwise it picks the cheapest priced nonfoil option (#527), even when that changes the art EDHREC and Scryfall show; the player can still choose any printing. Memorabilia (gold-bordered World Championship decks, Collectors' Edition, 30th Anniversary), oversized, digital and non-English printings are never picked automatically. Prices are Scryfall's daily estimates, so "cheapest" is not a purchase quote, and only the first eight cards in a batch load printings.

## Deck Doctor and simulations

`src/deck-doctor.ts` flags scant theme support, absent detected connections, and costs/pips high relative to averages/reported sources. Curve-only warnings require estimated cost at least eight and three above the spell average; six/seven-mana top end is not flagged on average alone. Broad matches protect unhelpful cards; missing tags flag useful ones. Its hybrid discount uses source counts, and simulation evidence uses mana-value-based turns and a draw-sample cutoff. Conditional sources hide problems; alternatives, reductions, and commanders cause misleading warnings. Never treat findings as automatic cuts, proof of no synergy/uncastability, mandatory casting turns, or confidence guarantees.

`src/deck-simulation.ts` samples the library (3,000 trials by default), excludes commander cards, draws on turn one, and marks decks under 100 cards provisional. It performs one redraw if the opening seven has fewer than two or more than five lands, then keeps that redraw. This is a model policy, not the Commander mulligan rule. It prefers untapped lands when choosing a land drop and may cast one affordable repeatable or land-ramp spell after the drop; the added source is available next turn. Its tapped-land check is a text heuristic. A nonproducing land can be played but cannot pay costs. A spell/land MDFC is used only as a land, never as both land and spell.

Costs support generic, ordinary coloured, and colourless symbols; hybrid, X, Phyrexian, and snow costs are reported unsupported. The model does not handle activation costs, conditional mana output, reductions, opponents, or full spell sequencing. It tests ordinary spells independently and does not cast or remove them. Spell estimates are conditional on drawing the card. `landDropRate` is the chance of a land play on that turn, not the chance of making every land drop through that turn. Never present samples as complete games, actual casting probabilities, strength, or brackets.

`scripts/recommendation-audit.ts` owns synthetic acceptance policies, fixture selection, and stopping limits. “Useful pick rate” measures policy acceptance, overlap measures reference names, and signature novelty is not relevance. Narrow fixtures/fabricated choices reward irrelevant cards or reject useful departures. Never present these as player acceptance, win rate, or recommendation quality.

## Functional staple offers (#591)

App heuristic, scoring version 10: Balanced and Deck needs first may swap one spell in the visible batch for an off-theme functional card when the batch number is divisible by four (Later-only cycles can jump past these numbers). Theme first, Surprise me and focused-role browsing are unchanged. Eligibility requires finite commander-specific EDHREC inclusion of at least 60%, a nonland card outside the local index, and a ramp, draw, removal or wipes role whose positive target is met but whose count is below target + 2. Highest inclusion wins, with queue order breaking ties. No card names are special-cased. Inclusion describes uploaded decks, not card quality.

The swap uses the filtered queue, respects the remaining deck budget, preserves lands, the creature slot when present and each selected theme's sole representative, and never replaces the first pick. A card already offered needs no swap. Zero targets receive no staple offers. Ignore, Later, price, ownership and set constraints still apply. Ordinary ranking may still offer cards above these bounds. The threshold, cadence and ceiling are uncalibrated app choices; calibration belongs to #98. See [measurement](../functional-staples-591.md).

### Role-aware land search (#590)

When Choose lands opens, and before building a starting deck, each active land role with fewer
than **3 eligible queue/Later candidates** gets one bounded Scryfall search. This threshold is an
app heuristic. Zero-weight fixing in mono-colour decks does not trigger a search. Earned roles
use #588's deck evidence; the returned lands still need its regex role match.

Queries combine `id<=<identity> legal:commander t:land` with a role's Oracle text/type terms
(and selected sets in Only-sets). They use no Tagger tags. One page, at most 175 cards, is requested
per deficient role, ordered by `edhrec`: popularity, never commander synergy. There are at most
eight searches per fill. The shared adapter owns pacing and its normal search cache; an additional
session cache shares pending results and remembers successful and empty lookups for repeated fills.
Cached results are rechecked against price, sets, ignored/deck cards and construction filters.
Failures silently retain queue-only planning. Search results say "Found by land search, not seen
with this commander" and lose ties at equal planner score to existing candidates. This is weaker
evidence, not proof of land quality. Broad role terms can miss less popular or unusually worded
lands; a single page is deliberately not exhaustive. At most nine eligible matches per role enter the queue. Failed lookups are evicted so a later fill can retry through the adapter's cooldown. Starting-deck searches use roles earned before its spell pass; roles earned only by newly added spells wait for a later fill.

## Starting-deck local theme filler (#619)

A starting deck takes at most three new nonland theme matches whose only provenance is a local-index snapshot, `Local card match`, or `In your collection` (including hydrated owned cards after reload). The cap applies across the theme and extra themes, including role-quota picks and Later candidates. Provider-backed candidates retain their existing order and filters; a missing EDHREC entry is not proof of a bad card. If candidates run out, the existing basic-land fallback can exceed the land target. Player-added cards and manual Add remain unrestricted.

**Voltron limit:** the tag matches `equipped creature`, `enchanted creature`, or `commander you control`. It does not distinguish beneficial enchanted-creature text from hindering or controlling text. Licids, bestow creatures and hostile Auras can count without being useful Voltron support. The cap limits speculative filler; it does not correct this classifier or validate provider-backed matches.

Provenance is limited by the queue merge: a hydrated `Local card match` may retain that reason even if a later provider response lists it, and remain capped. The cap uses stored provenance, not a fresh provider lookup.

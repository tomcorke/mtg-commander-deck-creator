# App heuristics

Checked 2026-10-03 against source `e7d6a1e`. These are current app choices, not validated Commander requirements. Rules, policy, and provider facts belong in [commander-rules.md](commander-rules.md), [format-policy.md](format-policy.md), and [data-sources.md](data-sources.md). A detected match is evidence to inspect; a missing match does not prove a missing interaction.

## Roles and editable targets

All five classifiers and their default targets live in `src/deck-analysis.ts`. Roles overlap, except that a wipe match prevents a targeted-removal match. The builder's counts include commanders and exclude sideboard cards. Targets are player preferences, not minimum construction requirements.

| Role and target         | Detection                                                                                                                            | Known false positives and negatives; never present as                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lands: **35**           | `Land` in the root type line or any face.                                                                                            | A transforming land back is not a freely playable land face. Spell/land MDFCs also supply spells. This count is not available land drops or a required land count.                                                                                                                                                |
| Ramp: **10**            | Nonlands with `produced_mana`, land-search/land-placement wording, `add {`, narrow Treasure creation, or additional land production. | Land Tax or Expedition Map can find a land without accelerating mana. Costs, conditions, and one-shot versus repeatable output are ignored. Additional land plays, cost reducers, unusual Treasure wording, and spell/land cards can be missed. This is not effective mana acceleration or a required ramp count. |
| Draw: **10**            | Selected literal `draw ... card(s)` phrases.                                                                                         | Opponent-only draw and cantrips can count without net advantage to the player. This is not total card advantage or a required draw count.                                                                                              |

## Curve, land range, and mana balance

These calculations live in `src/deck-analysis.ts` and are consumed by `src/app/useBuilderData.ts`.

- **Curve and type counts:** mana-value buckets are 0–6 and 7+, with fractional values floored for display. Land-classified non-MDFCs are omitted from the spell curve; MDFCs use a nonland face's type with the card's mana value. Types can overlap across faces. Display sections use priority ordering, not exclusive game types. Transform land backs can hide spells; multiple spell options and alternative costs can misrepresent actual demand. Never call the curve actual casting costs or an exhaustive playable-option breakdown.
- **Land range:** centre is `round(35 + 2*(average mana value - 3) - (ramp count - 10)/3)`, clamped to 32–40. The suggested interval is centre ±1, with outer guards at 30 and 42. Commanders contribute to the spell average. This treats unlike ramp cards equally and ignores draw, fixing, cost reduction, land modes, and strategy; it can over- or under-prescribe lands. Never present it as an official recommendation or an optimum.
- **Colour demand/supply:** demand counts coloured components of mana symbols across nonland faces; supply counts cards reporting each `produced_mana` colour. Alternatives can be counted together, and hybrid/Phyrexian pips can overstate mandatory coloured payment. Conditional sources and commanders can overstate early supply; multi-mana producers and reductions can be understated. Never call these available mana, casting probabilities, or identity validation.
- **Basic-land plan:** fill the editable land target within remaining deck slots; allocate basics proportionally to coloured pip demand, using equal shares if demand is zero and largest remainders for integer counts. No identity colours gives Wastes. Existing colour fixing, activation costs, and conditional demand are ignored; a missing chosen-colour state also makes the plan unreliable. Never call it a solved mana base or proof that a colourless choice is correct.

## Role urgency and warning timing

`src/deck-analysis.ts` uses urgency **1 / 2 / 3** below 70 cards, at 70–84, and at 85+. A role boost is the ceiling of its deficit fraction × **6** × urgency; lands/ramp get a **1.5** multiplier and cap **24**, other roles cap **18**. A zero target gives no boost. `src/app/recommendation-context.ts` applies these boosts only when deck-health priority is enabled.

Guidance starts at **70** cards. At **85+**, it becomes stronger when summed role gaps exceed remaining slots minus **2**. Overlapping roles can exaggerate slot pressure; important early gaps and misclassified functions can be missed. These are recommendation timing and weighting choices, never a deadline, legality failure, or evidence that a deck is unplayable. With Thematic health weighting disabled, `src/app/useBuilderData.ts` can offer up to **3** separate health suggestions from pending candidates; these remain detected-gap alternatives, not mandatory picks.

## Theme tags and suggestions

`src/domain/recommendation-themes.ts` matches text/type words for mechanics, creature types, keywords, and broader labels such as Group hug, Voltron, Big mana, and Political. It also adds plural card-type tags. EDHREC slugs map through hand-written aliases; unsupported slugs remain unsupported. Generic Tribal is detected but is not a selectable supported theme.

Words can describe an opponent, a cost, or an effect that does not support the player's plan. For example, `each player` need not be helpful Group hug, and `sacrifice` need not be a sacrifice engine. New mechanics, wording, and implicit interactions are missed. Never present a tag as an executed ability, a complete strategy classification, or an official theme definition.

A sub-theme is suggested after **3** matching tags among the last **12** noncommander main-deck selections, not after two related choices. `src/app/useBuilderData.ts` offers at most **1** inferred suggestion while fewer than **2** sub-themes are active; `src/domain/recommendation-queue.ts` limits active selections to two. Dismissed suggestions wait for increased support. Recency can exaggerate an incidental package or overlook an older one. Never call the trigger sufficient strategic support or a deck-building restriction.

`src/domain/commander-catalog.ts` holds curated theme/colour commander lists, theme search fragments, six named pair shortcuts, and three randomly chosen suggestions. `src/app/recommendation-actions.ts` supplements them with provider searches. Narrow text queries miss implicit themes; curated suggestions omit eligible commanders and valid pairs. `-is:commander` also excludes useful eligible cards from the 99, and random-comparator sorting is not uniform sampling. Never call these lists exhaustive eligibility, general pairing validation, or unbiased recommendations.

## Pairwise synergy clues

`src/domain/recommendation-themes.ts` returns the first regex pair matching one of six patterns: blink/ETB, token creation/payoffs, +1/+1 counters, sacrifice/death triggers, graveyard filling/recursion, or artifact-token creation/payoffs. `src/deck-doctor.ts` uses these clues when checking connections.

Ownership, targets, costs, timing, once-per-turn limits, and resulting resources are not evaluated. A match can require missing setup or affect the wrong player; real interactions outside these six patterns are missed. Never present a pair as a proven rules interaction, an infinite combo, or a bracket-combo detector.

## Signature seeds and evidence thresholds

`src/domain/signature-recommendations.ts` supports eleven engine families: counters, ETB/blink, sacrifice, tokens/populate, enchantments, artifacts, lifegain, graveyard/recursion, spellslinger, landfall, and equipment.

A profile needs **3 participants including the seed**: two other noncommander, nonland main-deck cards. Entries are counted, not necessarily distinct gameplay identities. Engine wording is separate from broad theme tags. Parenthetical text is stripped; opponent-triggered sentences are ignored for seed engine/multiplier tests. Equipped/enchanted death triggers and ordinary equipped-creature triggers do not establish sacrifice/equipment engines. This is a precision policy, not a claim that Smothering Tithe or Skullclamp cannot power a deck.

Candidates sort by selected theme/profile match (**2**, otherwise a relevant card tag **1**), multiplier status, then fit score. At most **3 seeds** are selected per pass. A qualifying ETB seed can use a commander page; other seeds use card pages. Profiles can select incidental text matches and reject real engines with different wording or unsupported mechanics. Never call selection a complete engine inventory or proof of synergy.

Evidence comes only from the ordinary creature, instant, sorcery, utility-artifact, enchantment, planeswalker, and battle lists. Each candidate needs **at least 100 observed decks** and either **raw lift ≥1.5** on card pages or **synergy >0** on commander pages. Card-page entries sort by deck count; commander-page entries sort by synergy. Names are deduplicated and capped at **24 per seed**, hence **72 per pass**. Truncated page lists, niche/new cards, and omitted list categories can hide good candidates; popularity and association can admit irrelevant ones. Lift 1.5 does not establish statistical significance or a rules interaction.

Incoming Energy or token-trigger candidates need **2** matching tagged peers; these counts can include commanders, unlike seed participation above. Creature-type-choice and Angel/Zombie-support wording is rejected; remaining checks use participants, counter-pair clues, or narrow sacrifice-fodder patterns. These screens can admit incoherent packages or reject useful typal cards. Never present them as necessary or sufficient support for those mechanics.

`src/app/signature-actions.ts` and `src/app/useSignatureRecommendations.ts` pause enrichment on complete decks, hidden tabs, and non-idle workflows. They wait **2 seconds** and allow, per rolling hour, **8 source attempts / 4 hydration POSTs per deck** and **24 / 12 per tab**. Actual background retries consume allowance; cached, cancelled-before-dispatch, and foreground-promoted work do not add a background charge. Transient backoff starts at **10 seconds**, doubles, adds up to **20%** jitter, caps at **5 minutes**, and respects provider cooldowns. These budgets can defer useful work and do not coordinate separate tabs. Never present them as provider allowances, relevance thresholds, or game timing.

## Fit scores, preferences, and batches

`src/domain/recommendation-types.ts` and `src/domain/recommendation-scoring.ts` define the 0–100 fit score.

| Factor                        | Current weights                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Evidence, max 20              | Commander synergy 20; commander favourite 15; popular inclusion or land/mana 8; other reasons 12. Thematic ×0.65; Competitive ×1.1, capped at 20; Fun adds 8 for Interesting new pick, capped at 20.                                                |
| Declared theme, max 10        | Matching tag +10; Competitive halves it.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Sub-themes, max 8             | +4 per match; Competitive halves it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Collection, max 12            | Matching sets: Only or Thematic +12; Fun/Balanced +8; Competitive +6.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Existing deck tags, max 10    | +5 per matching picked tag; Competitive halves it.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Preferences, range -10 to +10 | Sum learned tag/collection values; Thematic ×1.2, Competitive ×0.8; then clamp.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Deck needs, max 30            | Role urgency gets scarcity weight `1 + 4/max(1, supply)` and up to 12 additional batch-age points. Normalize to the strongest needed role, raise to 1.23, scale to 30. Thematic ×0.35; Competitive ×1.25. Without role boosts, +10 per needed role. |
| Mana-fit penalty, max 10      | Source deficit combines with cost above `max(4, average mana value + 1)`, reaching full cost pressure over 3 mana. Ordinary coloured-pip shortages can also penalize; slash symbols are ignored. Use the larger penalty, capped at 10.              |
| Popularity penalty, max 15    | Thematic subtracts 15 for commander favourites and popular inclusions.                                                                                                                                                                              |

The final sum floors at zero. Bands are **Low <25**, **Possible 25–<45**, **Recommended 45–<60**, **Strong 60–<75**, and **Top ≥75**. The visible Recommended badge goes only to the highest-scoring eligible visible card at **45+**. Source labels are qualitative categories, not measured interaction strength; list-taxonomy drift can change their weight. Overlapping tags can inflate scores, sparse tags can under-rank good cards, and aggregate mana counts can misjudge castability. None of these weights or bands is a win probability, card-power rating, bracket, cEDH verdict, or measure of fun.

`src/domain/recommendation-queue.ts` learns **+2** for Add, **-1** for Ignore, **+4** for More like this unless ignored, and zero for Later. These update tags and collection affinity, not the card's intrinsic quality. A liked addition can gain 6; shared generic tags spread that preference to unrelated cards. Unexpressed reasons for a choice are missed. Never present learned scores as verified player intent or card quality.

Batches contain up to **4** cards. Noncompetitive styles try for a creature when enabled, **3 non-mana picks plus 1 mana pick**, reason variety, and at most **1** Interesting new pick (**2** for Fun). Fallbacks can relax these preferences. Theme balancing tries to cover selected themes; Thematic tries for three identity-matching picks when supply permits. Creatures with mana penalties of **-5 or worse** are deprioritized but can return through fallback. Competitive omits these diversity quotas while retaining the optional creature preference. Quotas can displace a higher score or fail when supply is sparse; never present them as optimal deck composition or casting bans. `src/domain/recommendation-scoring.ts` labels root `Land` or literal `add {` text as Land or mana. It can miss land faces and indirect production, or include conditional output; this label is neither the ramp role nor available mana.

Later cards become eligible at batch number **+4**; undecided cards at **+3**. If no unseen cards remain, the queue can jump to the next eligible batch. These are queue cooldowns, never turns, elapsed-time waits, or judgments about usefulness.

## Power filters and collection affinity

`src/app/useRecommendationState.ts` defaults to Core (`precon`) and excludes Game Changers, tutors, extra turns, and unreleased printings. `src/app/useAppActions.ts` enables the first three exclusions for Core/Upgraded and disables them for High. The Core/Upgraded/High labels loosely correspond to Brackets 2/3/4, not verified deck classifications. Binary exclusions can over-filter an acceptable Upgraded deck or miss disallowed combos, land denial, loops, and intent. Never present settings as bracket compliance.

`src/domain/recommendation-scoring.ts` defines the Core-only `preconFastMana` list: **Chrome Mox, Grim Monolith, Jeweled Lotus, Lotus Petal, Mana Crypt, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond**. It omits accelerants such as Sol Ring, Ancient Tomb, and Lion's Eye Diamond, and includes cards currently banned outright. It is an incomplete preference list, never the official ban list, Game Changers list, or a complete definition of fast mana.

`src/domain/recommendation-sources.ts` uses literal `search your library` and `extra turn` text filters; Scryfall routes use `otag:tutor` and `otag:extra-turn` in `src/app/recommendation-actions.ts`. The routes are not equivalent. Land-search ramp can be excluded as a tutor; text mentioning prevention of extra turns can match without granting one. Other wording, indirect engines, and loops can escape detection. Never call these exhaustive tutor/extra-turn detection or a complete check of bracket guidelines.

`src/domain/recommendation-types.ts` curates Middle-earth as `ltr,ltc` and Marvel as `spm`. Matching selected set codes earns affinity; Only restricts candidates. This is not an owned-card inventory or a complete franchise taxonomy. Other sets and allowed alternate printings can be missed, particularly when a route checks only its hydrated printing; broad set membership can admit off-theme cards. Never present a match as ownership or exhaustive theme coverage.

## Deck Doctor signals

`src/deck-doctor.ts` reports weak theme support at **0 or 1** matching noncommander cards and weak connections when no selected tag, detected role, or known pair applies. Broad matches can protect unhelpful cards; missing classifications can flag useful ones. These are inspection prompts, never automatic cuts or proof of no synergy.

Mana outliers have coloured pips exceeding reported sources, or estimated cost **≥6 and ≥average +2**. The `{2/colour}` discount uses source counts rather than sampled payment options. When available, simulation evidence is shown at `ceil(mana value)`, clamped to turns **1–10**, only after **30 observed draws**. Conditional sources can hide problems; alternatives, reductions, and commanders can trigger misleading warnings. These are not mandatory casting turns, confidence guarantees, or proof of uncastability.

## Mana-access simulation and audit metrics

`src/deck-simulation.ts` runs **3,000 trials over 10 turns** by default, drawing seven opening cards from the current deck minus commanders. It chooses one detected land per turn, favouring immediate payability of held spells with weight **100**, then mana-type variety. It models only integer generic costs and `{W}{U}{B}{R}{G}{C}`. Spells are tested independently and never actually cast or removed; `whenDrawnByTurn` is conditional land-only payability. `landDropRate` is a successful land play on that turn, not cumulative lands in play.

There are no mulligans, ramp spells, tapped-land delays, activation costs, conditional production, cost reductions, hybrid/Phyrexian/snow/X costs, or opponent actions. Every detected land counts as one generic source, even a nonproducing land, and transforming land backs can be treated as playable lands. These assumptions can overestimate access; omitted ramp and alternative payment can underestimate it. Partial decks change the sampling population. The implementation also omits the first-turn draw, unlike normal multiplayer Commander. Never present these samples as a complete game simulation, actual spell-casting probability, deck strength, or a bracket verdict.

`scripts/recommendation-audit.ts` uses accept-all, type-count-balanced, precon-name-match, and all-ignore policies against four reference decks, stopping before batch number **500**. Its “useful pick rate” is policy acceptance per offer; overlap is name agreement with the reference deck. Narrow fixtures and fabricated choices can reward irrelevant inclusions or reject useful departures. Novel signature additions are likewise novelty, not relevance. Never present these metrics as player acceptance, win rate, or proof of recommendation quality.

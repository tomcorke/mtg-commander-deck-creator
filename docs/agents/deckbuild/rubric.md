# Completed-deck rubric (version 1)

Scores are heuristic desk estimates against the supplied spec. Confidence describes evidence quality, separately from the score. Use low when essential interactions or pace are uncertain, medium for partial evidence, high for well-supported conclusions. Record uncertainties and sources in the review.

| Criterion     | 1                                       | 2                                        | 3                                      | 4                                                      | 5                                                                      |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| bracketFit    | Clear mismatch with intended experience | Several material concerns                | Plausible fit with unresolved concerns | Good supported fit, minor concerns                     | Strong supported fit to intent and pace                                |
| effectiveness | No credible functional plan             | Major mana, resource or interaction gaps | Functional plan with identifiable gaps | Consistent plan with adequate resources and resilience | Cohesive plan with strong resource, interaction and resilience support |
| themeFit      | Theme barely represented                | Scattered theme cards                    | Theme meaningfully supported           | Theme drives most choices                              | Theme is coherent throughout                                           |
| ideaFit       | Contradicts the idea                    | Weak connection to idea                  | Idea recognisable                      | Idea strongly expressed                                | Choices consistently realise the stated story or concept               |
| fun           | Conflicts with requested experience     | Repeated experience concerns             | Plausibly enjoyable as requested       | Strong fit to requested play patterns                  | Especially compelling within the requested experience                  |
| synergy       | Pieces work against each other          | Isolated payoffs with little support     | Some functional enabler/payoff pairs   | Supported packages with protection                     | Multiple coherent packages reinforce the whole plan                    |

For effectiveness, discuss mana base, curve, draw, ramp, interaction, resilience and how the deck wins. For synergy, identify actual enablers, payoffs and protection rather than shared words. For fun, evaluate the spec's preferences; staples or repetition are not intrinsically bad.

For bracketFit, use the approved, dated format-policy topic at `E:/working/mtg-knowledge-base/knowledge/topics/commander/format-policy.md` and its Wizards sources. Discuss intent, pace, combos, extra-turn patterns, mass land denial and Game Changers, with explicit unknown coverage. Tutors alone are not a bracket restriction. App power presets and filters do not establish bracket compliance. Record under/appropriate/over/unresolved separately; a low score may mean either under or over. If evidence is insufficient, use unresolved and low confidence rather than claiming compliance. The operator must provide the topic as an approved reference; this relative link is not an isolation mechanism.
